## Design rules (from design-kit)

- **Tokens:** `src/kd/theme.css` is the only file that writes a hex colour; tokens are named for their role (`surface-0/1/2`, `line-1/2`, `fg-1/2/3`, `info`, `lime`, `on-accent`, `ok`/`warn`/`bad`). `npm run lint` runs `scripts/check-tokens.ts`: no hex elsewhere (a literal that must stay says `tokens-ok`), no transition with a time of its own (use `--dur-1/2/3`), and no more arbitrary `[Npx]` values than `PX_BUDGET`, which only goes down.
- **Colour says one sentence:** `info` is information (group names, links, the chosen tab or row, counts, "free"); the `--gradient-accent` is the one action a screen exists for (`buttonClass({ tone: 'acc' })`, `glow` on the one that leads the page); status is a dot with a word. Four button tones (`acc`, `default`, `ghost`, `danger`); radii 4 / 8 / 16 and the pill; glass only over a photo or a map; no drop shadows.
- **Lean UI:** titles and actions, no explaining lines, no tips cards, no line that counts results. Every string is an `{en, vi}` pair read with `pick()` / `tx()`; a new public line gets every other language of the site at once.
- **Fits the device:** no screen scrolls sideways at 390 / 768 / 1440 (`e2e/layout.spec.ts`); rows of chips and tabs wrap; tables fold into cards under 640px; everything one taps is 44 × 44px (`e2e/targets.spec.ts`; `kd-hit` on tappable text, `kd-tap` on small controls); no serious or critical axe violation (`e2e/wcag.spec.ts`); CLS ≤ 0.05 and the JS budget of each screen only goes down (`e2e/perf.spec.ts`). A new screen is added to `e2e/setup.ts` ROUTES.
- **Newcomer on a phone:** one screen, one job; three concepts first; one way to do each thing; fold, don't delete (secondary features under one "More" on a detail page or in "Me"). The first screen of the list and of a detail page may not gain controls (`e2e/first-screen.spec.ts`, budgets only go down); no internal name on a first screen; a missing value shows as "not given yet", never as a default.
- **Frame:** on a computer one bar (logo, four places, search ⌘K, language, one accent button, account); on a phone the logo and a search icon on top and a solid four-place tab bar at the bottom; "Post" lives in "Me" and the footer on a phone. Back offices: a rail of at most seven entries, tables that fold, drawers from the right, a "Today" list of to-dos with their actions.
- **One column:** no public page has a side column (rail, aside, sticky panel); what sat beside the content folds at its end, except what is needed at once, which sits next to what it explains. A page about one thing centres its column (a profile `max-w-page`, an article or a form `max-w-read`); lists stay full width. `e2e` checks `getByRole('complementary')` has count 0 and the level-2 headings come in the decided order (`design-system/patterns.md`).
- **Pull requests:** every PR that touches the front carries the "Thiết kế" checklist (`.github/pull_request_template.md`, checked by `design-checklist.yml`); `visual.yml` compares the main screens with the base branch and needs the `visual-ok` label after someone looked at `visual-diff`. Budgets never go up; a gate that blocks a good change is fixed in the same PR with the reason.
- **Owner decisions** are recorded here with their date ("owner 2026-10-18: …"); ask with 2–4 concrete options before changing one.
